Font Hierarchy

Two fonts, one system. Both available in Google Slides — no downloads required.

Primary: Quicksand

Slide Titles & Headlines 36–48pt Bold
Section Headers & Subtitles 20–28pt Medium
Supporting headlines and labels 14–18pt Regular
Use for: Slide titles, section headers, hero text, anywhere NEXT ››› appears.
Weights: Bold (700) for titles, Medium (500) for subtitles, Regular (400) for supporting headlines.
Google Slides: Available natively. Go to Fonts → More fonts → search "Quicksand."
Fallback: Nunito → Raleway → Arial Rounded MT Bold

Secondary: Inter

››› Chevrons & Data Match title size, ExtraBold
Body text, bullet points, and supporting copy 14–18pt Medium
Captions, footnotes, and data labels 10–12pt Regular
Use for: Body copy, bullet points, captions, data labels, and the ››› chevron symbol.
Weights: ExtraBold (800) for chevrons, Medium (500) for body, Regular (400) for captions.
Google Slides: Available natively. Go to Fonts → More fonts → search "Inter."
Fallback: Roboto → Open Sans → Arial

Quick Reference

Slide title Quicksand Bold — 36–48pt
Slide subtitle Quicksand Medium — 20–28pt
Body text Inter Medium — 14–18pt
Captions / labels Inter Regular — 10–12pt
››› chevron Inter ExtraBold — match title size
Tip: In Google Slides, click the font dropdown → "More fonts" → search by name. Once added, they stay in your font list for all presentations.
